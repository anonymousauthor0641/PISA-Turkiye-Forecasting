# Dataset

This folder contains the country-level datasets used in the Türkiye PISA forecasting study.

## Contents

- `01_raw_dataset/`: Officially reported national PISA mathematics and science literacy scores together with the socioeconomic indicators before missing-value completion.
- `02_processed_dataset/`: Final seven-cycle dataset after completing the missing socioeconomic observations.
- `03_future_inputs/`: Exploratory socioeconomic inputs for 2025, 2028, and 2031 generated by linear trend extrapolation from the completed 2003–2022 series.
- `data_dictionary.xlsx`: Definitions of the variables.
- `missing_value_report.xlsx`: Missing cycles and the completion procedures applied.

## Important interpretation note

The study uses officially reported OECD national mean indicators rather than student-level microdata. The future socioeconomic inputs are model-generated assumptions used for exploratory forecasting. They are not observed OECD values and should not be interpreted as known future socioeconomic conditions.
